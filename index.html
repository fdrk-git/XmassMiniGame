Hunt setup

    

      
1Write the sentence

      
The words the player spells out. Spaces split words; every other character (letters, numbers, !) is one hidden card.
         Up to 30 cards.

      
      

    

    

      
2Edit the hints

      
One hint for every step; the game shows it on a gift tag. What you type here is remembered on this computer only.
         The game reads hints as HTML, so a stray < or & would be misread.

      

    

    

      
3Record compass directions (optional)

      
Open this page on the iPhone. Stand at the starting point, hold the phone flat with its top pointing
         towards card 1, tap Record. Then stand at card 1 pointing at card 2, and so on. The last one is from the last card to the gift.
         Recording again overwrites a step; steps you skip keep what they had.

      

      
–

      

      

    

    

      
4Save the hunt

      
Copy this and paste it over the whole of js/config.js, then deploy. The game does not change until you do.

      

      

    

    

      
5Print the cards

      
Print this page (the instructions above are left out). The small number is the order.
